# Classify the subject, then its application

Use the actual objective and explanation to choose one primary path. A note tagged Quantitative Finance may teach general eigendecomposition; a note tagged Data Engineering may teach a kernel or a container. Put the general mechanism in its natural branch and connect its application separately. Domain names are practical navigation regions, not a claim that academic disciplines never overlap.

For the approved three-domain organization:

| Domain | Typical branches | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computing | Programming; Systems & Infrastructure; Data Engineering; AI & Machine Learning | Software, hardware, execution and data-processing mechanisms. |
| Mathematics & Statistics | Linear Algebra; Probability; Statistics; Stochastic Processes; Calculus; Optimization | General mathematical models and methods, including those illustrated with trading or ML examples. |
| Economics & Finance | Macroeconomics; Financial Markets; Market Microstructure; Quantitative Finance; Risk; Corporate Finance | Economic mechanisms, financial contracts, pricing, investment decisions and their operational controls. |

These are examples of meaningful children, not a checklist of branches to create. Keep empty speculative subtopics out of a content-organization pass; a deliberate assessment syllabus is a separate scope.

- **Computer Architecture** fits under Computing → Systems & Infrastructure, alongside operating systems, networking, distributed systems and cloud infrastructure. Do not keep Computer Systems and Computer Architecture as overlapping top-level regions.
- **Software Engineering** is not a top-level domain in this policy. Place the actual subject under programming, software design, testing, build/deployment systems or another concrete branch. A renamed or moved existing branch keeps its stable ID where possible.
- **Columnar storage**, **columnar memory** and **table formats** are related but distinct. A useful path is Computing → Data Engineering → Storage → Columnar Storage. Arrow's memory representation and Iceberg's table metadata should not be collapsed merely because both use columns.
- **Carry trades** are broader than their yen-funded application. A useful path is Economics & Finance → Financial Markets → Foreign Exchange → Carry Trades → Yen-Funded Trades. A currency, its monetary policy, and a particular funding strategy need separate placements when their content differs.
- **General methods versus applications:** eigendecomposition belongs in Mathematics & Statistics → Linear Algebra; portfolio allocation using eigenvectors belongs in Economics & Finance → Quantitative Finance. Use a crosslink to the method. A portfolio-risk simulation note can remain under Risk even though it uses Monte Carlo.
- **Ambiguous titles:** read the explanation before mapping “Iceberg.” Table snapshots and manifests describe Apache Iceberg; hidden reserve quantities describe an iceberg order. Similar names are not duplicate identities or evidence of a relationship.

Choose a broad supported branch when the content does not justify a narrower one. Preserve ambiguity explicitly and leave genuinely unresolved subjects awaiting classification rather than forcing a match. Multi-domain reference sheets can use a broad primary branch with related connections; cross-domain library guides can use the reserved note-only `library:guides` placement described in [the plan contract](plan.md). A navigation page does not establish an assessed learning objective.

A locked root policy is a guard against accidental top-level proliferation. Preserve it during routine authoring. Changing or opening the approved root set is an explicit organization decision, not a workaround for a failed plan. Use source-backed `explicit` connections for relationships established in content and `suggested` for inferred connections; neither kind duplicates evidence or changes expertise scores.
