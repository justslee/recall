# Audit

Read ../recall-source/references/quality.md. Inspect the entire requested scope, not a sample. Keep an objective inventory with source IDs, existing card IDs, separate math/code dispositions and specific reasons. Flag inaccuracies, incomplete paired definitions, contradictions, weak examples, generic visuals and semantic duplication. Distinguish safe additions from requested revisions. Route authorized authoring through specialist skills and validators. Report unresolved gaps; counts alone do not establish quality.
