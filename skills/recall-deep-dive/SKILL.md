# Deep Dive

Read ../recall-source/references/quality.md and knowledge.md. Start from the existing concept and the learner's question. Explain with a meaningful visual/example, ask a focused question, and respond to their actual answer. Do not fabricate learner responses. Record exposure separately from independent explanations or solutions. Preserve misconceptions and corrections with context. Reuse recall-cards for identified testable gaps and recall-self-test for enabled session capture; never adjust FSRS because a discussion happened.
