## Study experience

A local Mac study desk with optional visual exploration, broad topics, collections and focused questions.

Use the Study Index design: warm paper in light mode, quiet forest reading surfaces in dark mode, book-cover collections, layered index cards and translucent controls. Follow macOS appearance and keep the spacious coding editor in the same palette.

Preserve explicit answers and widget/code execution boundaries. Concept, math and coding formats follow their learning objectives; each keeps independent review state. No required hosting, account or cloud authoring service.

## Learning loop

Knowledge is a local reading, writing and retrieval space. Ordinary Markdown/HTML files remain the source of truth; a separate derived index supports low-latency lookup. Scoped authoring preserves unrelated metadata, rejects stale revisions and saves recoverable drafts. Notes connect to explicit practice objectives while Concepts, Math, Coding and Mixed remain distinct. Connecting a folder never migrates Notion or changes the canonical source. Imported notes cannot execute scripts or call privileged APIs. See [Knowledge space](KNOWLEDGE_SPACE.md).

The reusable learning loop includes conceptual teaching and KB creation, source-aware card authoring, purposeful visuals, independently checked math/code, opted-in capture and daily self-tests. Knowledge capture records exposure, not mastery. Recall's FSRS reviews own card scheduling.

Knowledge's Constellation keeps all domain roots on one continuous canvas. Branches expand and collapse in place, retaining context and explicit or suggested cross-connections. Bounded requests load the expanded graph automatically; there are no user-facing pages. Viewport-aware labels and canvas search keep large expansions navigable with keyboard controls as well as pinch and pan.

Domain color remains consistent down the primary hierarchy, and labeled context makes other domains recognizable while exploring a branch. Color expresses subject identity; particle density and review evidence express expertise.

Each profile can lock its approved top-level domains. Content is classified into meaningful broader-to-specific branches; imported tags do not become extra roots under a locked policy. An exact, unique existing label or alias can place a new item within a nested branch; ambiguous or unmatched items remain accessible in Unclassified. Library guides have a separate navigation shelf. The taxonomy skill preserves stable identities and relationships; scoring remains deterministic application code.

The provisional expertise index uses scored review evidence, separately exposes coverage and confidence, keeps unassessed requirements distinct from failures, and never reaches 100. Incremental SQLite projections update affected objectives and ancestor summaries without changing review history or scheduling. See [Constellation and expertise](CONSTELLATION.md).

Recall Pad separates live Study/Tutor sessions from private **Notebooks → Notes → Pages**. Notebook writing saves locally and is never automatically captured. An explicit **Send to Recall** shares a selected, immutable snapshot for source-aware KB and card preparation. Receipts distinguish durable receipt, preparation, actual destination outcomes and the last successfully added version; later edits remain private. See [Notebooks](NOTEBOOKS.md).

New notebook notes open straight into one growing sheet with pinch zoom, Pencil ink and editable shapes with attached labels. Additional sheets and existing multi-page notes remain accessible through an optional page tray. Autosave and explicit sharing preserve both handwriting and diagrams.

Notebook editing opens in **Write**. Direct tools make object selection and shape creation discoverable; a floating selected-object bar exposes Text, Style, Duplicate and Delete without shifting the sheet. Shape deletion is immediately undoable. Ink erasing and stroke selection remain distinct from object selection, with contextual properties instead of permanent editing panels.

Object selection exposes four resize corners and a top rotation handle, with a fixed opposite corner during resizing and text rotating with its shape. Lines and arrows support a single smooth midpoint bend; anchored endpoints follow rotated, moved or resized targets. Writing remains the default.

At every supported zoom level, the visible paper must remain writable without moving existing ink. Lines and arrows can attach to nearby container outlines, follow movement, rotation and resizing, and reconnect through visible endpoint handles. Existing free arrows stay free until explicitly attached; deleting a target keeps its arrow visible and Undo restores the relationship.

## Preservation and scope

Existing users retain content, identifiers, diagrams, attempts, schedules, drafts and sessions. Imports are additive and conflicts are explicit. New profiles have an optional original demo. [Recall Pad](HANDWRITING.md) adds an optional native iPad writing companion; library, feedback and scheduling remain on the Mac. Tutor Pad supports a [chat-bound paper-learning session](PAPER_LEARNING.md): publish a current prompt and read selected submitted pages through local assistant tools, with tutoring in the invoking chat. Manual copying into any application and iPad export/sharing remain available. Real-device recovery, transfer, intended-client image delivery and live grading verification are required before public distribution. Other platforms, general content-revision editing and background KB synchronization require separate implementation and verification.

## Quiet learning loop

Preparation must preserve the reader’s flow: existing ready objectives can be studied immediately, new work is bounded and resumable, and routine visual QA runs invisibly in disposable profiles. Setup belongs in one optional Connections hub. Progress opens to Atlas: bounded topic regions, focused ideas, format-specific evidence, week-ago comparison and explicit source relationships. Concepts, Math, Coding and Mixed remain separate for evidence and sessions. The existing review journal remains available; unknown evidence is never presented as mastery. See [the implemented workflow](LEARNING_FLOW.md).
