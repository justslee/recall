# Recall article kit

[Read the article](ARTICLE.md) · [Project README](../../README.md)

This is a draft for an X Article, with original illustrations. Nothing here publishes a post.

## Put it together

1. Use **AI helped me build faster. Was I actually learning?** as the title.
2. Upload the [cover PNG](../images/recall-article-cover.png) as the cover. Preview the crop before publishing.
3. Paste the [article text](ARTICLE.md), retaining the quote attribution and GitHub link. Upload the two figures at the image positions shown in the draft.
4. Preview on a phone as well as a larger screen. Upload the PNG files directly; local Markdown image links will not work in a published X Article.

X supports images and formatted text in Articles. Availability depends on the account's plan. [Official X Article guide](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/articles)

## Editorial notes

The opening admission, the question about speed versus understanding, and the closing question invite a real discussion. Avoid adding unsupported retention statistics or engagement bait. A useful reply can be a concrete example of something a reader shipped but could not explain.

Huberman says, “All of learning is anti-forgetting,” in the [official transcript](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/your-top-health-questions-answered). The article attributes those words to him; it does not claim he originated the underlying idea.

Product claims were checked against this checkout. Keep the beta availability sentence until public iPad distribution changes. Do not imply that learning capture is enabled by default, every chat is available to Recall, or all tutor clients have the same paper integration.
